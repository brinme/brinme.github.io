# Brinme 📦🚗

**Brinme** is a peer-to-peer platform connecting people who need to send packages with drivers traveling in that direction. Save on shipping or earn extra money on your trips!

## 🌟 Key Features

*   **Send Packages**: Find drivers traveling to your destination.
*   **Offer Rides**: Monetize your empty trunk space by delivering packages.
*   **Flexible Options**: Choose from Small (S), Medium (M), or Large (L) package sizes.
*   **Trust & Safety**: User profiles, ratings, and reviews.
*   **Telegram Ready**: Seamlessly integrated as a Telegram Mini App.

## 🚀 How to Use

1.  **Open the App**: [Brinme](https://brinme.pl) or open our [Telegram Bot](https://t.me/brinme_bot)
2.  **Sign Up**: Quick registration via Email or Telegram.
3.  **Start**:
    *   **Drivers**: Post your trip details (Route, Date, Capacity).
    *   **Senders**: Search for rides and send booking requests.
4.  **Connect**: Chat with the other party to arrange details.

## 🌍 Languages
Available in **English** and **Russian**.
